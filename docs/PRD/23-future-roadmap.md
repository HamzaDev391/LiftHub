23. Future Roadmap

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded from Version 1. Their inclusion in this roadmap does not represent a commitment to implementation order.

## 23.1 Member Features

Potential future additions:

• AI-assisted workout recommendations
• AI-generated routines
• Advanced progress analytics
• Body measurement tracking
• Progress photos
• Exercise video demonstrations
• Workout reminders
• Cloud backup improvements
• Wearable device integration
• Smartwatch companion app

---

## 23.2 Nutrition & Health

Potential future additions:

• Nutrition tracking
• Meal planning
• Water intake tracking
• Supplement tracking
• Sleep tracking
• Calorie estimation
• Macro tracking

---

## 23.3 Social Features

Potential future additions:

• Friends
• Workout sharing
• Challenges
• Leaderboards
• Activity feed
• Community groups

---

## 23.4 Trainer Features

Potential future additions:

• Client management
• Workout assignment
• Shared workout plans
• Progress monitoring
• Exercise feedback
• Trainer dashboard

---

## 23.5 Gym Management

Potential future additions:

• Member administration
• Membership billing
• Attendance
• Equipment management
• Staff management
• Business analytics
• Gym dashboard

---

## 23.6 Premium Features

Potential future additions:

• Subscription plans
• Advanced analytics
• AI-powered coaching
• Cloud exports
• Priority support

Roadmap Principle:

No roadmap feature should be implemented unless it strengthens LiftHub's primary mission of helping Members train consistently through fast and reliable workout tracking.
