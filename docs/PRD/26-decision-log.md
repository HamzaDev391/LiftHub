26. Decision Log

This section records significant product decisions made during planning and development.

The purpose of the Decision Log is to document not only what was decided, but also why the decision was made. This provides historical context for future contributors and helps prevent previously rejected ideas from being reconsidered without understanding the original reasoning.

Every significant product decision should be recorded before implementation begins.

Minor wording changes, formatting updates, and editorial corrections do not require a decision log entry.

## Decision Log Format

ID: DL-XXX

Date:
YYYY-MM-DD

Status:
Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected

Related Sections:
List affected PRD sections.

Decision:
Concise description of the decision.

Rationale:
Explain why this decision was made.

Alternatives Considered:
List any significant alternatives that were evaluated.

Design Impact:
Describe any implications for the ERD, architecture, UI, business rules, or future roadmap.
